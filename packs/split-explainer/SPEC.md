# Split Explainer, initial separate format

One original recording becomes a complete explanation using a lower presenter with upper visuals,
full visuals and full presenter as the argument requires. The format preserves concrete interfaces
and meaningful object behavior. Preferences vary its presentation, not its explanatory depth.

The cut foundation is copied from Talking Head 0.4.6. The format owns its planning, captions,
builder direction, three-mode composition, preferences and progress adapter. It ships no reference
creator footage and makes no changes to existing Talking Head projects.

The compositor supports an ordinary lower crop or a real source-aligned foreground matte with
a separate rounded room panel. Styled caption lines and timed runs are composited above the human.
It supports 1080x1920 at 30 fps, transparent graphics and continuous master audio through FFmpeg.
The optional local matting implementation currently requires macOS Vision.
The first export adds no sound effects or music. Effects are a separate choice after delivery.
Preparation normalizes camera cadence to 30 fps without changing playback speed. An explicit
whole-take choice produces an identity timeline instead of pretending that a cut was approved.
Progress reaches the shared runtime task reader; a dedicated new-format app route and native
preview integration have not been established by this prototype.

Version 0.1.0 is an experimental starter. A technical composition test does not establish creative
quality on a fresh recording or mean it is ready for sale. The complete skill explains the
necessary planner, six-to-eight-builder, inspection and revision process for that evaluation.
