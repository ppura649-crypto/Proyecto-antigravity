# Facts, dinosaur-extinction (checked 2026-10-29)

- 66 million years ago, ~12-km asteroid, Yucatán coast of Mexico (~180 km Chicxulub crater), steep ~60-degree impact angle (45°–60° to horizontal), worst-case trajectory maximizing ejected rock and sulfur: Collins et al. (2020), *Nature Communications* 11, 1480. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-15269-x
- 2024 ruthenium isotopes in K-Pg clay proved carbonaceous C-type asteroid from beyond Jupiter: Fischer-Gödde et al. (2024), *Science* 385(6710), 752–756. https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk4868
- 2023 *Nature Geoscience* study: micrometer silicate dust (0.8–8.0 µm), trapped in atmosphere up to 15 years, cooled Earth up to 15 °C, shut down global photosynthesis for nearly 2 years (~730 days), ~75% species extinction: Senel et al. (2023), *Nature Geoscience* 16, 1033–1040. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41561-023-01290-4
- Fossil fish at Tanis, North Dakota with impact glass spherules in gills proved impact struck in Northern Hemisphere spring (boreal spring / austral autumn): During et al. (2022), *Nature* 603, 91–94. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04446-1
