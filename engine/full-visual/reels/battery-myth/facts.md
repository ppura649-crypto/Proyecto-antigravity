# Facts: Battery Overnight Charging Myth (Checked 2026-10-30)

1. **1990s NiCd vs. Modern Lithium-Ion Cutoff & Optimized Charging (80% / 100%):**
   - Source: Apple Support, "About Optimized Battery Charging on your iPhone" (`https://support.apple.com/en-us/108055`)
   - Verified claim: On-device machine learning delays charging past **80%** overnight until just before the user wakes up, and lithium-ion power management integrated circuits (PMIC) stop charging current once **100%** capacity is reached, unlike 1990s nickel-cadmium packs susceptible to overcharge stress.

2. **Permanent Thermal Damage Threshold (`35 °C / 95 °F`) & Insulating Cases:**
   - Source: Apple Support, "Batteries - Maximizing Performance" (`https://www.apple.com/batteries/maximizing-performance/`)
   - Verbatim fact: Avoid exposing your device to ambient temperatures higher than **35° C (95° F)**, which can permanently damage battery capacity; charging inside certain cases may generate excess heat, so remove the case if the device gets hot while charging.

3. **Depth of Discharge (`0%` ~400 cycles vs. Partial `20%–80%` >1,000 cycles):**
   - Source: Battery University, "BU-808: How to Prolong Lithium-based Batteries" (`https://batteryuniversity.com/article/bu-808-how-to-prolong-lithium-based-batteries`)
   - Verified claim: Consumer lithium-ion cells subjected to **100% Depth of Discharge** (draining down to `0%`) deliver **300 to 500 cycles** (~400 cycles average), whereas partial discharges in mid State-of-Charge windows (`20%–80%`) exceed **1,000+ cycles** of usable lifespan.
