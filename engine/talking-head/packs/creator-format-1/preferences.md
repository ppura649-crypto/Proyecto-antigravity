# Creator Format 1 pack: what this creator likes

High level only. Rewritten when a pattern in their reactions becomes clear, never appended to as
a log. The skill reads this before the plan; anything measured (fonts, sizes, positions) lives
in `formats/creator-format-1.json`, anything about sound files in `sfx.md`.

**Visuals.** Full illustrations drawn with real detail are their favourite thing, and a chart for
any number the creator says. The creator wants every idea to get its own picture, on the word, and would rather
see a bold drawn object than a screenshot. Black plates behind dense diagrams, real artifacts on
a light card, marks floating bare. Square corners, one accent colour, their words on every label.
The creator notices when a visual stops before the creator has finished the thought, and when two look alike.

**Captions.** Creator Format 1's grammar: words as they are spoken, the hero word big and in the accent, the
title on top for the first seconds in plain white with no plate behind it. Nothing sped up.

**Sound.** Sparse, gentle, on the instant that matters, from their palette in `sfx.md`. The
everyday accents a touch louder than instinct. The creator dislikes any sound under a movement in
progress and any sound on a transition; a wrong sound is removed, not swapped.
