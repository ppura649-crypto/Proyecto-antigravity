The score is a floor, not a formula: 01 against 05 shows that the verb and the twist matter more than any test. Say
the hook out loud and ask the one real question: would a viewer stop mid-scroll?

### Formulas with slots

- **Verdict:** "[daily tool] is just [label], let me tell you exactly why:" The label must be proved by at least two
  proof units, not only the last third.
- **Myth-bust:** "If you think [the scary line], you're wrong, and I'm gonna prove it to you." Sentence two carries
  the first number.
- **Threat to your thing:** "Your [thing] can [event with an enemy] [round number] before [the moment you think you
  are safe], and it can be fixed with [one small thing] that I'll give you at the end." Use an event (sued, hacked,
  billed), not an obligation (owe, taxed).
- **Discovery:** "[Named company] just [built / proved / published] [specific thing], and [hard number]." Name the
  thing within 5 s: "It's called ...".
- **Prohibition + count:** "Don't [what the viewer is doing] unless you've [done these N things]." The count is the
  loop; every item pays one.

## Units

**Item unit (risks, myths):** your thing, what happens, the number with its source, what it means for you. 20-35
words, 6-10 s.
> "Your fonts load straight from Google's servers, and a court in Munich made a site pay €100 to a single visitor,
> just for leaking their IP address."
> "You texted your waitlist about the launch. Without written consent, that's $500 per text under federal law."

**Tool unit:** "The Nth is NAME, which VERBs ..., [number]." Add one sentence with a trigger moment only when the
behaviour needs showing.
> "The second is OmniRoute, which gives Claude Code almost unlimited usage by connecting it to over 300 other free AI
> providers. So the moment that your usage limit runs out, it'll automatically switch you to the next best model..."

**Step unit (a story, an incident):** "Okay, step one: [goal]." then the cause and its consequence.
> "Okay, step one: get out of the sandbox. That package server was allowed online, just to download packages. So they
> made it download anything they wanted, and it became their window to the internet."

**The last unit is different:** "Even the profile pictures your users upload are a risk...", "And finally, there's
Agent Skills...". Escalate or change the sentence shape.

## Mechanism lines worth copying

- "which turns your entire codebase into a knowledge graph, so your agent doesn't have to waste tokens by reading
  files again and again." (what it does, then why you care)
- "So instead of one model auditing its own code, you get a second opinion from the smartest model on the planet."
  (instead of X, Y; check "smartest" before you ship a line like it)
- "Your landing page is a static site, so it can't cost anything, until someone hits it with a DDoS attack." (belief,
  then "until")
- "Budget alerts don't stop anything." (the false comfort, alone)
- "which in California counts as wiretapping at $5,000 per session." (re-label with a word that hurts)
- "Every computer processes that audio signal a little differently... We're talking about tiny differences. Nothing
  you'd ever notice, but enough to create a fingerprint for your computer." (scale line)
- "So they started leaving messages in folder names on it, like notes in a shared locker." (analogy in the sentence)

## Turns (one per reel, before the CTA)

- "The word is per. Nothing on this list is a percent of revenue. It's per visitor, per session, per email." (names
  the pattern with the facts: the best turn in the set)
- "None of this is a fine. It's money you already owed on day one." (restates the hook: weaker)
- "These agents weren't trying to take over. They were just trying to pass a test. And that's what makes it scary."
  (a reframe; stronger with a number in it)
- "Unfortunately, Chrome and Safari don't offer anything comparable by default. So switch browsers." (the verdict
  paid, then an instruction)

## CTAs

- `So if you want every source, just comment "WATER", and I'll send them to you.`
- `Comment "SWARM" and I'll send you the full reports.`
- `So if you want to try them all out for yourselves, just comment "CODING", and I'll send the link to you directly.`
- `Comment "SPLIT", and I'll send you a link to the skill.`

One topic word, what arrives named exactly, and it must exist before posting. No save line, no "link in bio", no
second ask stacked on top unless the creator asks for one.
