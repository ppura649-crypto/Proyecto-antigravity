# Keyframes

Short videos for your account, made on your machine by the agent you already use.

## Set up, once

1. Unzip this folder anywhere you like.
2. Open the folder in your agent (Claude Code, Codex, Grok, Cursor or Gemini CLI) and say: set me up.
3. The agent shares your local workspace and installs what your formats need. Account-based formats can then read your own posts.

If you bought Product Demo, you can start with a product URL, repository or recordings. Tell your agent which product the film is for. Your agent handles the machine setup; a social account is optional.

## Every day

Open your workspace and choose a new video. Add footage and copy the prompt into your agent. You can also say <!-- keyframes:commands -->`/full-visual`, `/talking-head` or `/split-explainer`<!-- /keyframes:commands -->. Your saved look stays saved. Finished posts land in `workspace/output/`, with the video, caption and cover. Post from your phone.

## If something goes wrong

Say: doctor. It checks the machine and fixes what it can. If you still need help, ask for the support message and it writes one.

## What never happens

Nothing is posted for you. No other account is read. Your original clips are never modified. Your keys stay in `.env.local` on your machine.
